Changes in Go Updating Go Version Support Policy Go Protobuf is updating its Go version support policy to support the two most recent major releases of Go, aligning with the official Go Release Policy and Google Cloud Go’s supported versions policy. Previously, Go Protobuf supported the two most recent Go releases plus one additional prior release to align with Google Cloud Go’s policy at the time. Now that Google Cloud Go has updated its policy to support the two most recent Go releases, Go Protobuf is aligning its support window accordingly.
