TL;DR: We are planning to release Protobuf Editions to the open source project in the second half of 2023. While there is no requirement to move from proto2/proto3 syntax to Editions syntax at initial release, we encourage you to plan a move in your software project’s future timeline. Protobuf Editions Protobuf Editions replace the proto2 and proto3 designations that we have used for Protocol Buffers.
